Hand & Finger Placement

🤚 Left Hand

Little finger: Q, A, Z
Ring finger: W, S, X
Middle finger: E, D, C
Index finger: R, F, V, T, G, B

🤚 Right Hand

Index finger: Y, H, N, U, J, M
Middle finger: I, K, comma
Ring finger: O, L, full stop
Little finger: P, semicolon, slash, Enter

Home row position: Place left fingers on A S D F and right fingers on J K L ;. Keep both thumbs near the Spacebar. Return to the home row after reaching for other keys.

Important: A normal laptop keyboard cannot tell which physical finger you used. TypePro can show the recommended finger and check the character you typed, but it cannot verify your actual finger movement.
